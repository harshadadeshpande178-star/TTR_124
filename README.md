# ⚡ KINETIC // Student Consistency & Discipline Platform

An all-in-one web platform engineered specifically for students to build uncompromising daily consistency, beginning from the brutal morning wake-up call to the final 30-minute victory music session.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Implementation Overview

### 1. 🖤 Ultra-Dark Black Aesthetics & Starting Emojis
- **AMOLED Deep Black Theme** (`#08080a`, `#101015`, `#14141c`) with glassmorphism card surfaces and glowing neon accents.
- Eye-catching hero section featuring animated starting emojis (`🚀 ⚡ 🎯 📚 🔥`).
- Motivational daily quotes and real-time clock counter.

---

### 2. 🚨 Unstoppable Morning Alarm Guard
Standard alarms allow snoozing, leading to sleep inertia. The **KINETIC Alarm Guard** *cannot be shut down* unless the student completes one of two challenges:
- **Option 1: Walk 100 Real Steps 👟**:
  - Step counter with radial progress and physical stride buttons.
- **Option 2: Candy Crush Match-3 (2 Stages) 🍬**:
  - Fully playable 8x8 Match-3 puzzle board with candies (`🔴 🔵 🟢 🟡 🟣 🟠`).
  - Swap adjacent candies, cascade gravity, and score points.
  - **Stage 1 Target**: 500 points.
  - **Stage 2 Target**: 1,000 points.
  - Once Stage 2 is cleared, the alarm automatically silences and unlocks your day!

---

### 3. 🪜 Step-by-Step To-Do Roadmap
Tasks are laid out as a sequential journey of steps:
- **Completed Tasks**: Represented with a **vibrant Blue color** (`#3b82f6` glowing border & accents) and an **exciting emoji** at the end (e.g. `🎉`, `🚀`, `🔥`, `🥳`).
- **Pending Tasks**: Represented with an **alert Yellow color** (`#eab308` border) and a **stressed emoji** at the end (e.g. `😰`, `⏳`, `🤯`, `😬`).
- **Dual Mode Flexibility**:
  - **Sequential Stepper Mode**: Follows strict cognitive science order (Step 1 $\rightarrow$ Step 2 $\rightarrow$ Step 3).
  - **"Complete Remaining Later" Mode**: Allows students to defer tasks, with dynamic completion percentage calculation (e.g., 50%, 60%, 75%).

---

### 4. 🪙 Credit Points & Discipline Overtime Penalty
- **Completion Reward**: $+5$ credits per completed task step.
- **Failure Penalty**: $-10$ credits deducted per failed/abandoned task.
- **-50 Point Overtime Penalty Trigger ⚠️**:
  - If a student accumulates 50 points in net deductions, the system triggers the **Overtime Study Protocol**: an urgent warning banner appears requiring **2 extra hours of study** with a live countdown timer.

---

### 5. 💡 Smart Study Sequence & 5-Question Quiz Gate
- Displays the optimal cognitive study roadmap (Morning wakeup $\rightarrow$ Concept learning $\rightarrow$ 5-10 practice questions $\rightarrow$ Milestone quiz $\rightarrow$ Nutrition $\rightarrow$ Sports $\rightarrow$ Social $\rightarrow$ Music reward).
- Recommends **5 to 10 Practice Questions** for every task.
- **Milestone 5-Question Quiz Gate**:
  - Interactive quiz covering calculus, physics, chemistry, computer science, and neuroscience.
  - **Gating Mechanism**: The student *cannot advance past active steps* without completing the 5-question quiz!

---

### 6. 💧 Water Intake & Hydration Fuel
- Visual interactive water bottle with fluid wave animations.
- Tracks daily target of **3,000 ml** (8-10 glasses).
- Quick add buttons (`+250ml`, `+500ml`) with percentage fill overlays.

---

### 7. 🚴 Daily Physical Activity Selector
- Rotate between 4 student-endorsed brain-oxygenating activities:
  - 🚴 **Cycling** (45 mins, ~350 kcal)
  - 🏃 **Running** (35 mins, ~420 kcal)
  - 🏊 **Swimming** (40 mins, ~400 kcal)
  - 🧘 **Yoga** (35 mins, ~220 kcal)
- Includes completion tracking ($+5$ credits) and workout timer.

---

### 8. 🥗 Nutrition Chart & Complete Student Diet Plan
- **Macro Fulfillment Dashboard**: Live progress bars for **Protein** (75g target), **Complex Carbs** (220g target), **Fiber** (30g target), and **Healthy Fats** (50g target).
- **Full Student Diet Chart Generator**:
  - 07:30 AM: Power Breakfast
  - 11:00 AM: Brain Booster Focus Snack
  - 01:30 PM: Balanced Brain Lunch
  - 05:00 PM: Afternoon Recharge
  - 08:00 PM: Recovery Light Dinner
  - 10:00 PM: Circadian Sleep Inducer (Turmeric Milk)

---

### 9. 👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Friends Wellbeing + 📚 Growth Reading
- **Social Connection Checklist**: Dedicated daily 30-minute block to talk with parents and close friends to lower exam stress and improve emotional regulation.
- Rotating conversation starters.
- **Personal Development Reading**: Curated non-fiction books (*Atomic Habits*, *Deep Work*, *Make It Stick*, *Can't Hurt Me*) with reading timer and key takeaways.

---

### 10. 🌙 Circadian Sleep Tracker (90-Min REM Cycles)
- Computes ideal bedtime based on the morning alarm (5 cycles = 7.5 hours, 6 cycles = 9 hours).
- Sleep quality metrics and 30-minute wind-down checklist.

---

### 11. 🎧 30-Minute Victory Music Session Reward
- Automatically unlocked when **100% of the day's activities and tasks are completed**.
- Built-in Web Audio API synthesizer for lo-fi beats, synthwave, ambient flow, and victory anthems.
- 30-minute session countdown timer and dancing audio visualizer.

---

### 12. 👑 7-Day Unbroken Consistency Streak: "FREE DAY" Award
- Visual 7-day progress tracker (Monday to Sunday).
- Completing a full week without failing tasks awards the student a **100% FREE DAY** pass.
- Curated guilt-free leisure activities: movies, gaming marathons, cheat meals, nature hikes, and creative hobbies!

---

## 🚀 How to Run
Simply open `index.html` in any web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).
No server or build process required! All data is saved automatically in `localStorage`.
